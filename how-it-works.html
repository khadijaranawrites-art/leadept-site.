The LEADEPT method

How we turn LinkedIn into booked meetings.

No guesswork, no black box. Four clear stages — Strategy, Content, Outreach and Calls — run for you over a focused 120-day program, with you in the loop the whole way.

Step 01 — Strategy

First, we get sharp on who you want and what makes them lean in.

Vague positioning is why most LinkedIn effort fails. Before a single word goes out, we lock the foundations.

  • Ideal customer. Exactly who you want on your calendar — role, company, and buying trigger.
  • Positioning & message. The angle that makes you the obvious choice, not one of many.
  • Target research. A verified list of the people worth reaching.
1

What you walk away with

A one-page game plan — your target buyer, your core message, and the offer every conversation drives toward. The compass for everything that follows.

Step 02 — Content

Then we build your authority so buyers arrive already warm.

We ghostwrite and publish thought-leadership posts in your voice. By the time we reach out, your name already carries weight.

  • Your tone of voice. We capture how you think and turn it into posts that sound like you.
  • Consistent cadence. A steady drumbeat of high-signal content — no more posting once and going quiet.
  • You approve everything. Nothing publishes without your sign-off. It's your name.
2

What you walk away with

A living feed that makes you look like the leader you are — content that sells before you ever speak, and a growing audience of the exact people you want as clients.

Step 03 — Outreach

Next, we start real conversations with your ideal buyers.

Personalised, human outreach — never mass-blasted spam. We reach the right people and open genuine dialogue.

  • Precise targeting. Your exact ideal customers — not whoever happens to be nearby.
  • Personal, not robotic. Every message is relevant to the person receiving it. That's why people reply.
  • We handle follow-up. The fortune is in the follow-up — done consistently so nothing slips.
3

What you walk away with

A steady flow of warm conversations with qualified prospects — managed for you, so your inbox fills with the right people instead of noise.

Step 04 — Calls

Finally, qualified meetings land on your calendar. You close.

Everything points to one outcome: the right people, booked into your calendar, ready to talk.

  • Booked for you. We move conversations to the calendar so you spend time selling, not scheduling.
  • Qualified, not random. Prospects who fit your offer, so your calls are worth showing up to.
  • Clear reporting. Every week: what went out, what came back, what's booked. Full transparency.
4

What you walk away with

A predictable pipeline. Instead of hoping referrals show up, a repeatable engine puts qualified meetings on your calendar — month after month.

Why a 120-day program

Real business relationships take a little time.

The best pipelines are built, not bought overnight. Four focused months is what it takes to compound.

  • Trust builds through consistency. Valuable interactions, repeated, are what move people to act.
  • The algorithm learns your audience. Reach and relevance improve the longer we run.
  • Your audience needs to see you. People engage once you've shown up enough to be worth their reply.
Good questions

The things founders ask us first.

How much of my time does this take?

A short interview up front, then brief weekly approvals. We do the heavy lifting; you stay in control without living on LinkedIn.

Will it actually sound like me?

Yes. We capture how you think and write, and nothing publishes without your approval. Prospects can smell a template — we don't use them.

Is this just spammy automation?

No. We run personalised, relevant outreach built around real targeting. The goal is genuine conversations, not blasting strangers.

What if it doesn't work?

If you're not landing 5–15 qualified meetings monthly within 120 days, we keep working for free until you do. The risk is ours.
