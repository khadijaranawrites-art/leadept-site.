The LEADEPT method

How we turn your LinkedIn into booked calls.

No guesswork and no black box. Four clear stages — Strategy, Content, Outreach and Calls — run for you end-to-end, with you always in the loop.

Book a call → Walk me through it
Step 01 — Strategy

First, we get sharp on who you want and what makes them lean in.

Before a single word goes out, we lock the foundations. Vague positioning is why most LinkedIn effort fails — so we start here.

  • Ideal customer. We define exactly who you want on your calendar — role, company, and the moment they're ready to buy.
  • Positioning & message. We nail the angle that makes you the obvious choice, not one of many.
  • Offer clarity. We make sure the thing you're inviting people to is easy to say yes to.
1

What you walk away with

A one-page game plan: your target buyer, your core message, and the exact offer we'll drive every conversation toward. This is the compass for everything that follows.

Step 02 — Content

Then we build your authority so buyers arrive already warm.

We ghostwrite and publish content in your voice that earns trust at scale. By the time we reach out, your name already carries weight.

  • Written in your voice. We interview you, capture how you actually think, and turn it into posts that sound like you.
  • Consistent cadence. A steady drumbeat of high-signal content — no more posting once and going quiet for a month.
  • You approve everything. Nothing goes live without your sign-off. It's your name — you stay in control.
2

What you walk away with

A living feed that makes you look like the leader you are — content that does the selling before you ever speak, and a growing audience of the exact people you want as clients.

Step 03 — Outreach

Next, we start real conversations with your ideal buyers.

Personalised, human outreach — never mass-blasted spam. We reach the right people and open genuine dialogue that leads somewhere.

  • Precise targeting. We build lists of your exact ideal customers — not whoever happens to be nearby.
  • Personal, not robotic. Every message is relevant to the person receiving it. That's why people reply.
  • We handle the follow-up. The fortune is in the follow-up — and we do it consistently so nothing slips.
3

What you walk away with

A steady flow of warm conversations with qualified prospects — managed for you, so your inbox fills with the right people instead of noise.

Step 04 — Calls

Finally, qualified calls land on your calendar. You close.

All of it points to one outcome: the right people, booked into your calendar, ready to talk. You show up and do what you do best.

  • Booked for you. We move conversations to the calendar so you spend time selling, not scheduling.
  • Qualified, not random. We focus on prospects who fit your offer, so your calls are worth showing up to.
  • Clear reporting. Every week you see what went out, what came back, and what's booked. Full transparency.
4

What you walk away with

A predictable pipeline. Instead of hoping referrals show up, you have a repeatable engine putting qualified calls on your calendar — week after week.

Good questions

How much of my time does this take?

A short interview up front, then brief weekly approvals. We do the heavy lifting; you stay in control without living on LinkedIn.

Yes. We capture how you think and write, and nothing publishes without your approval. Prospects can smell a template — we don't use them.

Is this just spammy automation?

No. We run personalised, relevant outreach built around real targeting. The goal is genuine conversations, not blasting hundreds of strangers.

How soon will I see results?

Early conversations often start within the first few weeks as content and outreach ramp. We'll set clear, honest expectations on your intro call.
